## 1. The One-Paragraph Proof Statement

> **"I build machine learning ranking and decision-support models that work on messy, real-world search data, evaluated honestly under client-holdout validation with zero data leakage. I am proving this to an engineering lead at an applied AI or search intelligence company who needs reliable, production-ready prototypes, so they will email me to schedule an interview."**

---

## 2. The One-Line Why

> **"A resume or LinkedIn profile only lists machine learning buzzwords and course certificates; this portfolio proves I can take 79 million rows of messy, pseudonymized search data, hunt data leakage, and deliver an auditable decision-support system that beats human rules by nearly 3x."**

---

## 3. Breakdown Against Evaluation Criteria

* **The ONE Claim:** Building machine learning ranking and decision-support models on real-world search data, validated honestly with zero data leakage. *(Single core skill; avoids the "and" trap).*
* **The ONE Person:** An engineering lead or hiring manager at an applied AI or search intelligence company who needs practical, production-ready prototypes. *(Specific decision-maker, not "everyone").*
* **The ONE Action:** Email me to schedule an interview or discuss an applied ML role. *(Single unambiguous call to action).*
* **Specificity:** Tailored specifically to applied search ranking, holdout client validation, and real-world tabular data constraints.
